🎬 52–60% of anime comes from manga

Manga is the backbone of the anime industry. But studios can only adapt a fraction — mega-hits like Jujutsu Kaisen and My Hero Academia absorb all the budgets, leaving thousands of stories with no audio identity.

📚 The gap is only growing

New title releases surged 6.5% to over 16,000 in a single cycle — the largest output expansion since 2008. Audiences are reading faster than studios can adapt. The silence is getting louder.

The Solution

A soundtrack for every panel, generated automatically

Drop in a comic page. C.O.S.M.I.C detects the panels, understands each one, and composes a custom three-layer soundtrack — then plays the page back panel by panel, like a motion comic.
